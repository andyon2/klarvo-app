### 4. Reader quality note

Reader B was the more precise reader in this subsystem: its line numbers were within ±3 everywhere, it correctly separated cloud from local for H6/H7 (B13), caught the `strip_stockphrase_ghosts` side-of-cleanup regression (B12) and the `parseFloat||0` reachability (B24); its two misses are a wrong *cause* in B23 (device-open latency is excluded by the post-ready stamp) and an unsupported mechanism in B13 (echo/fragment leaks cannot occur on a prompt-less local path — the real offline loss is the dictionary `initial_prompt` and the ghost strip). Reader A was accurate on the config-contract rows (A3/A4/A7/A8/A9/A14) and its A6 wording ("a single term ≥10 chars, or the exact comma-list") turned out exactly right on simulation, but it systematically cited `groq_jni.rs` three lines early (`jni:177-179`/`213-216`/`230-234` → actual 180-182/214-219/234-238), under-rated A24 as MEDIUM although effect (a) drops whole dictations at defaults, and rated A14 "med" on the key question where the code shows the upload happens key or no key. Shared blind spots: neither reader noticed the inverted guard order in the JNI (V-STT-1), the 429-vs-4xx classification gap (V-STT-2), or the orphaned-WAV/no-pending-row degrade on Android's local path (V-STT-3); both declared the Android Silero threshold unverifiable although the AAR sits in `~/.gradle`.
