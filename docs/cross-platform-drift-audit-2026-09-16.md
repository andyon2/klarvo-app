## Headline

1. **The license paywall exists on Android only.** Desktop's hotkey dictation pipeline has no license check; the one command-level LLM gate reads a field the runtime never uses. After trial expiry the same config keeps dictating through OpenAI Whisper / DeepSeek / OpenRouter on Desktop and silently drops to Groq on Android — and the two platforms do not even agree what the free tier is (Desktop: DeepSeek + Groq; Android: Groq only). June's C1 is not fixed; its direction reversed. (D-C1, D-C2, D-H1, D-H2)
2. **Android's "Local (Offline)" cleanup cannot execute at all** — the native library is never built or packaged, and the model path does not match the downloader — and `cleanupLocal` returns the raw transcript **as a success**. Both readers described this as "a weaker prompt". (D-H18)
3. **Desktop has no banking / password-app guard**; Android has one but writes the transcript to `history.db` and Turso before the guard blocks the paste. (D-H3)
4. **STT conditioning drifted into a new shape.** Since ADR-0017 both platforms build the Whisper prompt in Rust — but Android passes the *LLM cleanup instruction* as the prompt, the echo/fragment guards on Android see the dictionary terms as "prompt" (short dictionary-word utterances are dropped, a single ≥10-byte term is deleted from every transcript), the guards run in inverted order, and the stockphrase-ghost strip sits on the wrong side of cleanup. (D-H4…D-H8, D-M9)
5. **Six settable-on-Android controls are dead there:** Translate-to, STT model, offline Whisper model, the three STT-prompt boxes, App Profiles, Auto-Send, Silence Duration (the Android slider writes keys no Android mode reads; the keys that matter are `isDesktop`-gated). (D-H12…D-H17)
6. **20 of June's rows are fixed** (chunking unit, provider fallback, energy gate, highpass, dictionary-in-Chat, the whole dead-config cluster, live preview …). **17 persist, 9 changed shape, 1 reversed.** See §6.

Counts after verification: **CRITICAL 2 · HIGH 20 · MEDIUM 24 · LOW 32**; 6 reader claims refuted or reclassified as non-divergences; 22 new rows came from the verifiers' recall sweeps — none of them from either discovery reader.
